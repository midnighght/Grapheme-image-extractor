# Symbol image requirements

This document describes the conditions that each letter, number or symbol image must meet.

## Technical format

- **File format:** `.png`.
- **Size:** 128 × 128 pixels.
- **Colors:** black and white only, no shades of gray.
- **Background:** black.
- **Stroke:** white (the symbol is white on a black background).
- **Position:** the symbol must be centered in the image.

## Writing rules on the template

Letters, numbers and symbols must follow the guidelines on the template:

1. The letter, number or symbol must stay within the upper and lower limits.
2. **Lowercase letters** go from the baseline to the lowercase line, except for letters with ascenders and descenders (see the list below).
3. **Uppercase letters** and **numbers** go from the baseline to the uppercase line.
4. **Symbols** go according to where lowercase letters would go.

## Letters with ascenders and descenders

These lowercase letters are the exception to the lowercase rule, because they extend above the lowercase line or below the baseline. They must still stay within the upper and lower limits.

| Type | Letters | Where they extend |
|---|---|---|
| Ascenders | `b` `d` `f` `h` `k` `l` `t` | Above the lowercase line |
| Descenders | `g` `j` `p` `q` `y` | Below the baseline |

## Letters with diacritics

Letters with diacritics (vowels with an acute accent and vowels with a diaeresis) are **optional**. As agreed in class, recognizing them is not mandatory, but they were included in the template in case anyone wants to include them.

## How to use the template

* Print it on an A4 sheet.
* Fill in the boxes with the indicated symbols.
* Scan the sheet.
* Crop the image as shown on the next page.
* Upload it to [Grapheme image extractor](https://midnighght.github.io/Grapheme-image-extractor/) and download your processed, ready-to-use images.
